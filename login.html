PRO TIP
Cybersecurity Tip of the Day

Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) & Use a Password Manager: Over 80% of data breaches involve compromised or reused passwords. Enabling MFA (preferably using an authenticator app or hardware FIDO2 key rather than SMS) blocks more than 99% of bulk automated credential attacks according to CISA and Microsoft threat intelligence. Pair this with unique, generated 16+ character passphrases for every account.
